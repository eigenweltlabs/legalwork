## How billing guidelines work

A private guideline belongs to the signed-in profile on this device. Firm, Client and Matter specify which work it covers. A firm guideline in this list applies to that person’s drafting across matters. A published firm policy is managed by an authorized billing administrator.

The service looks up eligible versions using the **service date**, selects one effective version at each applicable scope and resolves rules by field. A client guideline is available during initial reconstruction when the matter identifies a single visible billing client. For a matter with several billing clients, an explicit recipient identifies the client guideline during invoice wording review. Use a matter guideline for an instruction common to that matter’s recipients.

A guideline can change descriptions, terminology, proposed billable increments and wording checks during invoice review. For example, 17 recorded minutes with a six-minute round-up can yield a proposed 18 billable minutes. The booking service validates binding billing rules when the user accepts. Rules do not issue invoices, change hourly rates in a fee agreement or rearrange the time table.

The records-backed service supports synchronized time entries and existing invoice attachments. Full approval, pricing and issuance flows require a firm service that implements those operations. That distinction affects the actions visible in Billing settings and Invoices.

The [user guide](../user-guide/time-tracking.md) covers linking a guideline, reviewing a suggestion and working with either billing service.
