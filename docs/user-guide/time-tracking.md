# Time tracking and billing

## Which features does my firm support?

Open the clock below **Back Office**. **Time entries** (or **My time**) combines saved entries and private AI suggestions in one editable table. Choose the date and **Day** or **Week**. **Week** shows a total for each day. Select a day in those totals to change which day **Day timeline** shows. **Suggestions · n** hides or shows suggestions without accepting or deleting them. Expand **Activity sources** to choose inputs, or **Day timeline** to inspect clock ranges for the selected date. **Invoices** (or **Billing review**) and **Billing settings** remain separate tabs.

In a firm using ordinary Back Office records, type into the bottom row or click its **+**. The **Date** cell defaults to today, or to the day you selected above the table. You can change it or clear it to return to the selected day. Choose a timekeeper, a matter, a description, recorded minutes and chargeable minutes. **Tab** commits the cell and moves right. **Shift+Tab** commits and moves left. A complete row saves automatically and a new empty row appears below it.

With **Unsaved changes** showing, return to the draft’s date to finish it or choose **Discard changes**. A local server keeps incomplete rows in private device storage. With a remote-only connection, drafts stay available as you move between dates and pages during your session. The app warns before closing or reloading with unsaved work. Use **Try again** beside a failed save. If another editor changed the same field, compare the values beside the row and choose **Keep saved value** or **Use my edit**. Changes to other fields still save.

The timekeeper picker lists members from the connected organization and defaults to **Me**. Choose a colleague to enter their time. The person filter defaults to yourself. **All people** shows accessible entries for others. **Copy row** creates a private draft for the selected date under your own name and clears the invoice link and clock range. To copy to another day, focus a cell in the original row and press **⌘C / Ctrl+C**, select the target date, then paste in the table with **⌘V / Ctrl+V**. Review the copy and commit a cell with Tab to save the completed row. The history icon opens the existing record history. Use **Options** to change columns and save a table view to reuse it. For a matter with several billing clients, show the **Client** column and select the intended billing client. If members cannot be loaded, reconnect and choose **Try again**.

Suggestion rows use the same columns with a second line identifying the source and any measured duration or project. Edit the cells directly. Those changes remain private until you choose **Accept suggestion** on the left. **Dismiss suggestion** removes the suggestion. Click the source line to inspect it and choose **Correct** to change or split the time. When a suggestion matches one of your accessible entries on the same day and matter, **Already recorded…** links the two and keeps the source private. The totals include saved time.

The small saving indicator replaces the storage banners. Its tooltip explains sharing. **Billing settings > Billing in your firm** links to **Fee agreements** and explains any unavailable approval service. The ordinary table uses the same records as **Back Office > More > Time entries**. Partner approval, work-product tracking, grouped meeting allocations and project stopwatch-rounding overrides require the timesheet service. A connection or login error remains an error; it does not silently select another storage mode.

Enter the actual **Service date** for chargeable work. A date and recorded minutes are enough for duration-only work. If you have a genuine clock range, keep it in **Started at** and **Ended at**. An organization-member link identifies whose work the record describes. The colleague confirms it in their own timesheet when the firm supports that workflow.

To store an existing invoice PDF, open **Time tracking > Invoices**, open the invoice, choose **Invoice PDF** and attach the file, then save the field. The file is versioned and linked to this invoice and its matter. Use a file up to 10 MB. The existing Back Office synchronization uploads the file before publishing its record reference. Attaching a PDF does not generate, number, approve or issue the invoice. If there is no **Invoices** tab, you need billing access. The automatic issue-and-attach-PDF flow requires additional server support; it is not implemented by the directory-only mode.

## Where is my time saved, and who can see it?

| Stage | Storage and visibility |
| --- | --- |
| Stopwatch, source events, AI suggestions and original proposal versions | Private workday storage on the device running the local LegalWork server. Not sent to the firm by preparing or correcting a proposal. Connected calendars/mail are read from their providers; the optional drafting model runs locally. |
| Edited suggestion cells, incomplete ordinary rows and copied rows | Saved in the account’s private workday storage on the local device after committed edits. They do not enter the shared record queue until accepted or completed. With a remote-only connection, unfinished manual cells remain in the open app; private capture is unavailable. |
| A saved ordinary Back Office entry in a connected firm | Saved in the local sync queue, then sent to the firm automatically. Offline or failed sync means it is pending, not permanently private. Default sharing allows billing members with access to the matter to see even Draft records. The current table has no private-before-submission mode; Draft status does not restrict sharing. The row's owner is the creating account; a timekeeper contact is not an authenticated colleague's confirmation. |
| A booked entry with the full timesheet approval service | Sent to that service when booked, before submission. The workflow separates the worker's entries from the assigned reviewer's submitted queue. Source originals remain private; sharing a billing comparison is a separate opt-in. |
| An entry in the local directory | Saved on the LegalWork server serving that directory (on this device for a desktop installation). The generic Back Office first-sync/publishing controls are separate from submitting a timesheet. |

**Draft does not mean local-only.** Storage location, record sharing and approval status are separate. Back Office's existing history records saved revisions; private source storage is not an independently witnessed audit service. The private workday file uses operating-system access permissions, not application-level encryption. People with access to that device account or its backups may be able to read it. A firm administrator's technical server access is separate from what the app exposes to reviewers.

The following sections describe the full timesheet workflow available in the local directory or on a firm server that implements it.

## Enter, copy and correct time

Choose the clock directly below **Back Office** in the left navigation. Open **My time**, select a work date, then use **Day** or **Week** to change the period. Choose an earlier date to enter yesterday’s work. The small figures above the list show worked time, billable and non-billable work, and the chargeable quantity after billing adjustments.

Click the empty row or the **+** below the last saved entry. Type a client name, matter or file reference, choose an activity, and enter a narrative and minutes. Tab advances right and Shift+Tab moves left. Tab out of the last cell, press Enter on a completed new row, or leave its completed charge cell to book it automatically. The next row keeps the date and matter. Save the open row or press Escape before switching dates or submitting the period. DFT creates a work product. MOD suggests products already recorded for that matter and accepts a new title. If two documents have the same title, select the specific result using its author, creation date or ID. To start a second document under an existing title, use **+ (Create a separate work product)** in the Work product cell, enter the title and book the row. A copied row keeps its original product ID. Use **Columns** to show or hide date, timekeeper, work product, actual minutes and the two time allocations. The selection is saved for this directory and user on this device. **Default view** restores the original columns.

For a meeting, select the first timekeeper in the input row. To record only a colleague’s time, choose their name here. Use **Add person** below the row for each additional participant. Enter billable and non-billable minutes for each person. For example, enter 30 billable and 10 non-billable minutes for yourself and 20 billable minutes for a colleague. Each person gets their own 40- or 20-minute line under the same meeting. The chargeable quantity can be rounded or written down independently. Tab out of the completed charge cell or press Enter to save everyone together. **Entered for others** shows your original entries for colleagues in the selected period. Your totals count only your own confirmed work.

In a connected firm, each participant confirms their own line before submitting. On a single device, open **Back Office > People**, create a person, choose an internal type such as **Associate**, and enable **Active**. That person then appears in the timekeeper list. A local internal contact can be named on a line, but confirmation by another person requires a connected firm account. Mixed time and individual participant durations require a firm server that supports them.

To reuse a whole row, select it using its row selector, press **⌘C / Ctrl+C**, choose the target day, then **⌘V / Ctrl+V** in the list. Edit the draft before saving. It starts under the current worker and target date and retains the matter, work product, activity, narrative and durations. A new copy has its own identity and history. You can also duplicate the selected row from the toolbar. Click an editable cell to correct a saved line in place. Tab or Enter saves a changed cell. Escape discards the pending edit. Changing a submitted line returns it to your list for resubmission. Opening and closing it without changes preserves its submitted status. The history action shows the previous and current values.

## Reconstruct a workday

From **Time tracking**, expand **Activity sources** above the table. The view is available on the local LegalWork server. Time tracking opens independently of chat subscription and provider setup. Use the switch beside each source you want, such as **Project calendar** or **Completed dated tasks**. Select the date and choose **Refresh activities**. A task without a known duration leaves the duration blank. Proposals appear directly as editable rows in the table. **Accept suggestion** creates the billing record. Dismissing a proposal excludes it from subsequent preparations for that date.

In the desktop app, open the profile menu and choose **Show mini bar**. Hover over the floating microphone to reveal **Dictate**, **Record meeting** and the stopwatch. Click the stopwatch to open its project panel. It suggests the project shown in the last active LegalWork window. Select another project if needed. From a screen outside a project, choose one from the list. The panel stays inside the mini bar overlay; scroll the project list to reach further projects. Click **Start stopwatch**, then move the pointer away to collapse the bar. A small green dot shows that the clock is running. Click the stopwatch again to see the running time and project. Changing the project assigns the entire running interval to that project. Click **Stop stopwatch and save time** to create a private interval. The clock keeps running when you close and reopen the app.

In the time table, select the date and edit the stopwatch suggestion to assign a matter, describe the work and book it. You can use the stopwatch with all automatic sources disabled. A run spanning midnight appears on each affected day. In the ordinary firm-record table, both **Recorded minutes** and **Chargeable minutes** are filled automatically from the stopwatch and its rounding rule, including short runs. The source row retains the measured duration. In the full workflow, actual worked minutes remain separate and a sub-minute interval still requires a whole worked minute before booking.

Stopwatch entries propose a rounded invoice quantity. The ordinary firm-record table also uses this rounded duration for **Recorded minutes**. The original measured start, end and worked time remain available in the private source after you accept the row. The stopwatch rounds up to the applicable billing increment by default. To change one project, open **Time tracking > Billing settings**, select **Project** and the project, choose the effective work date, and turn **Time rules > Round stopwatch time up** off. Choose **Save billing settings**. With this setting off, the stopwatch rounds to the nearest increment and rounds exact halves up. With a 10-minute increment, 13 minutes proposes 20 with the setting on and 10 with it off. A 17-minute interval proposes 20 in both modes. The calculation uses the exact interval, so 10 minutes and 1 second rounds up to 20. The stopwatch suggests quantities even when **Enforce billing increment** is off. That setting controls which invoice quantities you may enter manually. The general **Proposed rounding** setting controls other entries. The stopwatch uses the applicable rule when you change the matter, project or work date on a suggestion, and when you correct or split a measured interval or edit a billable allocation. Charge amounts you enter manually take precedence. Fully non-billable work has zero charge.

The time axis shows timed proposals by matter. Recorded work has its own column. The time table also includes proposals without a time range. If a suggestion overlaps another suggestion or time you already booked, correct the time range before choosing **Accept suggestion**. Use **Already recorded…** if the suggestion describes work you already entered for that date and matter. **Correct** lets you adjust the description, matter, clock range or duration. **Move part to another matter** assigns the final minutes to a second matter and previews both actual durations and charge options. Save the correction and book each part separately. You can compare both parts with the private original in entry history. For spoken text, use a keyboard dictation mode configured for offline processing on the device.

To include a connected source, first connect and activate the account in **Integrations**. Enable its source separately in **Time entries > Activity sources**. **Google primary calendar** and **Outlook primary calendar** read calendar titles and times for the selected date. **Gmail sent messages** and **Outlook sent messages** read sent-message subjects. A mail reminder has no duration or assigned client. Enter those in the list before saving. Disconnecting or deactivating an account hides its reminders. Workday currently has no operating-system-wide active-window collector on macOS or Windows.

Use **Activity sources > Connect calendars and email** to open Integrations. **Edit AI instructions** opens Workflows. The interface calls the drafting action **Suggest with AI**. Processing continues on the device as specified for private activity data. For model-assisted drafting, configure the local server environment with absolute paths in `LEGALWORK_TIMESHEET_LLAMA_CLI` (llama.cpp CLI) and `LEGALWORK_TIMESHEET_GGUF` (local text-generation model). Restart the server, then choose **Suggest with AI**. **Workflows > Suggest Timesheet** controls the drafting instructions. The runner reads the installed workflow for each run and uses the model in offline mode. A 15-, 30- or 60-minute interval prepares the current workday while the local server runs. Unconfirmed narratives and the original proposal versions stay in this device's private workday storage. The confirmed entry carries its proposal digest. The calendar can also be used without installing a model.

## Set billing rules, review fees and issue invoices

Open the **Billing settings** tab in **Time tracking**. You can also open a saved client or matter in Back Office and choose **Reviewer and billing rules**. In Billing review, selecting a client or matter shows the same link. Project Home has a **Billing settings** link for that project. The **Responsibility and approval** section appears first and shows the reviewer for the selected scope. Set the effective date to the work date you intend to review. Changing billing settings, including reviewer assignments and exceptions, requires Back Office model administration, record editing and finance access. An assigned reviewer with finance and edit access can review submitted work. Choose firm, client, matter or project and an effective date. A project can inherit its client’s increment or set an override. **Enforce billing increment** applies to each positive chargeable line. At ten minutes, a line can be 10, 20 or 30 minutes. Disabling enforcement allows individual minute amounts. Actual time is kept separately. **Proposed rounding** can suggest the next, previous or nearest unit in the charge cell. The default leaves the choice to the worker. A line above the applicable activity limit can be saved and submitted in full. It is marked for review until the charge or applicable exception is resolved. Expand **Time rules > Activity exceptions** to set a separate limit for an activity. The settings below group invoice wording, review and fees. Change the language, wording, rates, currency or reviewer there, then choose **Save billing settings**. At firm level, **Activity codes and labels** adds firm-specific choices to the time-entry list. Saved revisions retain the label in force at the time. **Opening words by activity** specifies how a description begins. **Preferred wording, spelling and abbreviations** supplies replacements. During review, accept or dismiss a proposed correction beside the submitted wording. For one-person local practice, explicitly enable solo approval before approving your own work. For a connected firm, the platform must support the timesheet service and authenticated approval.

If several fee agreements apply to the same client and matter, you can record the work while the fee is unresolved. In **Project Home > Project details**, select the intended fee agreement. If your project has no agreement field, open **Customize fields**, add a Back Office field for **Fee agreements**, save it, then select the agreement. In **My time**, choose that project in the client/matter cell before submitting. A flat-fee or RVG agreement without an hourly allocation shows the worked time with the fee unresolved.

Imported time entries use the billing increment in force on the service date. Historical entries marked **Submitted** still need the worker’s signed submission in **My time > Submit period**. When an import names a linked colleague, the line first appears in that colleague’s **My time** for confirmation. If you created an entry whose timekeeper cannot be matched to a firm account, expand **Imported entries that need a timekeeper** in **My time** and open the entry. Choose the linked person under **Timekeeper**. For a record that describes your own work, you can clear **Timekeeper** to use your account. Save the field and return to **My time**.

On the device where you accepted a workday suggestion, open **My time > Original comparisons for this submission** and choose **Compare with private proposal** beside the line. Compare the suggested activity, description and worked time with your first saved and current versions. Select **Share this billing comparison with my reviewer** for each original you wish to disclose, then choose **Submit period**. Each new submission starts with sharing off. You can submit without a comparison if the original is unavailable. In **Billing review**, expand the row and its history to see a shared comparison or the **Private** label. Calendar accounts, clock ranges and event identifiers stay on the device. The local directory supports this option. In a connected firm it appears when the firm server supports billing comparisons. You can always submit without sharing an original.


To correct a previously approved or invoiced entry, open its own history in **My time > Request correction**. Enter the new description or chargeable minutes and a reason, then choose **Send for approval**. The submitted request keeps the original and worked duration intact.

Submit the entries in the personal list. In **Time > Billing review**, select a matter and period, filter to wording or billing exceptions, and click the narrative or charge to edit it. If another reviewer changes the line while you are editing it, press Escape and reopen the cell to review the current version. Open the row history to compare the submitted version and review changes. Select the exact rows to approve. The footer gives the selected fees, grouped by currency. The work-product view shows contributions across workers visible to the reviewer. Expand a work product to rename it, choose its type or add a document or email reference while retaining all linked entries. New products retain their creation date and author. Open **Adjustments** in Billing review to compare the original and requested terms. Approve or reject the change with the amount in view. An approved post-issue adjustment creates a separate downloadable note linked to the original invoice.

To check the current client wording rules, select submitted positions and choose **Check billing text**. Review the applicable rule and compare each current description with its suggested replacement. Choose **Apply previewed changes** to save the displayed wording changes together. If a line or rule changed, check the selection again. The revised positions remain in the review queue for approval.

Before approving hourly billable time, open **Pricing** on a flagged row to see what is missing. Set the hourly rate and currency for the service date in **Billing settings**, or complete the applicable fee agreement in Back Office. Choose **Return to contributor** so the contributor can resubmit the entry with the new terms. If the entry was already approved without a price, select it in **Approved**, enter a reason and choose **Return for pricing review**. Resolve any pending corrections first. The contributor can then use **My time > Submit period** to send it back for approval. The original approval is available in the row history. Time under a flat-fee or RVG agreement can still be approved for tracking. Those entries show their fee as unresolved and stay out of hourly invoices.

In **Billing settings > Firm > Invoice template**, set the document title, language, letterhead and payment footer. In **Billing review**, choose approved entries for one client, matter and currency, inspect the preview and issue the invoice. The preview uses the approved wording, quantities and rates, including any approved corrections. Changing billing settings applies to new reviews. A number is assigned at issuance. Reopen the issued HTML document from the invoices list. For newly issued invoices, the **Net total** in Back Office uses the same currency precision as the issued document, such as three decimal places for Kuwaiti dinars or whole Japanese yen.
