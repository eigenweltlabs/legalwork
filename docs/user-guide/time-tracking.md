# Time tracking and billing

## Enter time and reconstruct a day

Open **Time tracking → Time entries** (called **My time** in the full workflow). The default workspace shows your booked work on the left and the selected day's timeline on the right. Each pane has the same tab strip as Wiki, aligned with its content. Choose **+** (**New tab**) to open **Time entries**, **Day timeline** or **Private chat**. If the tab is already open, choosing it brings it into view. Use the tab’s **×** to close it. You can reopen it from **+**, including after closing every tab. Drag the divider to resize panes. Drag tabs to reorder or move them, or open a tab’s context menu to move it to another pane or split the pane. With a tab focused, Shift+Left or Shift+Right changes its position. Use Ctrl+Tab to switch tabs in the focused pane. On a small screen, **+** also takes you to tabs in another pane. Choose **+ → Reset workspace layout** to restore the initial arrangement. In **Week** and **Month**, the first figure is recorded time and the second is billable time. Select a date to open its daily entries and timeline.

Choose **+ New entry** at the left of the list. Enter the date, matter, service description and duration. A clock range is optional. The client usually follows from the matter. Choose the recipient when several clients apply. A complete manual entry follows the ordinary save and synchronization path. When another window changes the same private draft field, resolve the reported conflict before retrying the save. A row created for another date stays editable under **Just created** while you finish this visit, including after its first save. You can finish its wording and billable time without switching dates. Its time belongs to its service date, and the row appears there when you return. Use the row selector and the usual copy and paste shortcuts to reuse an entry.

Opening Time entries automatically reconstructs the selected day and places proposals in **Day timeline**. The agent considers the enabled event streams together and can combine, split or omit events. Use the **Timeline settings** gear to choose the model and activity sources. Automatic model selection prefers Eigenwelt Sonnet, then Haiku or Luna, and otherwise an available model. A saved model selection takes precedence. The optional schedule updates the current day. A connected model processes the selected evidence and relevant billing context. Private evidence, the conversation and rejected proposals stay outside the shared timesheet. A configured local model can process these on the device. If the model fails, select **Try again** in the workday to request a new draft for the same day with the selected model.

The timeline uses one color per matter and a separate color for unassigned work. Use **Timeline layout → By matter** for separate lanes. Untimed work appears separately. The red line marks the current time today. To review a suggestion with the keyboard, focus its calendar block and press Enter. Tab moves through the editor. Close the suggestion or press Escape to return to the timeline. The AI can group several events into one suggestion, divide work across matters or leave irrelevant activity out. Review the suggested narrative, matter, activity code, recorded time and billable time before accepting. Each calendar block has a shared pill with a cross on the left to reject it and a check on the right to book it. When required details are missing or times overlap, the check opens the editor for review. Use the existing-entry action when the same work was booked manually.

Saved edits update the calendar block’s description, matter and color. Enter **Recorded** minutes directly in the suggestion editor, including for a document without a known duration. The edit stays private until you accept the suggestion. If you change its duration, the booking becomes an entry without clock times; the original event remains available as source evidence. **Edit source and times** lets you specify an exact interval instead. Correcting the source preserves unrelated edits, such as a client selection or reviewed billable time. Changing the duration, matter or activity recalculates the fields that depend on that change. If another window changes a draft before you correct or accept it, review the updated values and confirm again. Text you are still typing stays in the editor during a background refresh.

Opening or returning to a workday, changing the selected day, changing model or source settings, and stopping a timer request a fresh reconstruction. The assistant reuses an existing result when its evidence and context have not changed. New activities do not each trigger a model call. To refresh today's activity while the app stays open, choose a 15-, 30- or 60-minute interval in Timeline settings. The local service must be running. The view's one-minute background reload only retrieves saved suggestions. After a failed model request, use **Try again**.

Open **Stopwatch** in the Time entries toolbar, choose a project or **No project**, and select **Start stopwatch**. Open it again to change the project or select **Stop stopwatch**. Its timer is shared with the floating desktop stopwatch. In Timeline settings, **Always show** offers a stopped timer for manual review. **Use as an AI source** lets the model combine the timer with other activity. On Home, activity follows the project selected in the composer. Choosing the general assistant ends the foreground observation for the previous project. An idle window and a running project agent provide separate observations. Completing a task in the desktop's task view provides evidence on the completion day, including for tasks without a due date.

Use **Private chat** to change the current draft. Unsent text and its instruction scope are kept separately for each account and day while the app is open, including when you leave the workspace and return. Open the composer options to save an instruction for future days or inspect saved instructions. Pick its personal, matter or document scope. A document-specific correction such as “Call this document Revision” can guide later amendments. Creating an eligible project document provides DFT evidence and saving later changes provides MOD evidence. The model uses the effective wording rules, document history and available evidence when drafting the actual client-facing description.

When you correct and confirm a document-linked suggestion, the private assistant remembers your wording for that document and matter. Editing the wording of its saved entry can also update this memory. Up to four confirmed examples guide later suggestions, including on the same day and after the document is renamed. The assistant uses the examples for terminology and style, not as evidence that the past work happened again. In the Private chat composer options, open saved instructions to edit or remove **Learned wording**. These examples stay on this device under your account.

Accepting unchanged AI wording does not create a new learned correction. Full document history additionally requires confirmed workflow entries linked to the document's work product. A firm that exposes ordinary records only does not supply that history; private human corrections and uploaded guidelines can still guide the wording.

## Billing settings and access

In the basic **Time entries** view, you can continue recording for yourself when the people list cannot load. Your own complete entries enter the local outbox when the firm connection is interrupted and synchronize when it returns. Recording for another person still requires the firm's current permission; a previously loaded people list does not grant access during an outage.

**Billing settings** contains the rules used to review and price entries, including effective dates, rates, increments, language and terminology. In **Timeline settings**, import billing instructions from TXT, DOCX, searchable PDF or structured JSON. For text documents, the selected model proposes numerical rules such as minimum time and billing increments, with source quotations. Review the text and the **Time rules** fields, correct any mistakes, then choose **Save guideline version** to save a dated version for a client or matter. Nothing is saved by extraction alone. For pasted text or a retry, choose **Extract time rules**. If extraction fails, retry or enter the rules yourself. Fields omitted by a new extraction retain their existing values. **Structured rules (JSON)** exposes the additional rule fields as JSON.

Device-only instructions belong to that account. A published policy takes precedence for fields it explicitly sets; unrelated published settings, such as the reviewer, do not erase uploaded wording guidance. Binding client terms and authorized exceptions remain governed by the firm. Centrally managed billing policy and delegation depend on your firm's service.

For example, a reviewed six-minute rounding rule can suggest **17 Recorded / 18 Billable** without changing the actual duration. Acceptance also checks the billing service's rules. If that service requires ten-minute increments, the suggestion cannot be booked as 18 minutes: have the authorized billing administrator correct the applicable policy or review the proposed billable time. A private guideline does not change the service's policy. After resolving a failed acceptance, **Try again** retries that acceptance; retrying a failed private edit keeps the suggestion private.

Each saved entry has its own status. With the full workflow, submit a period from **My time**. An authorized reviewer can check hours, pricing, manual wording and suggested corrections. Issuing a bill links the included entries to the invoice. Approved changes to a previously issued entry use a correction linked to the original invoice.

## Create an invoice

In the full workflow, open **Billing review → Create Invoice** and choose the matter. Select a service period or include open work through a cutoff date. This includes older entries that became eligible after an earlier invoice. Resolve missing prices, approvals and competing fee agreements before issuing. A temporary connection failure keeps the open editor and its inputs available. Use **Try again** to reconnect. A change of account or access permissions requires a fresh authorized view. When several billing clients apply, choose the recipient explicitly.

For an hourly invoice, choose the billing currency when the matter has fees in several currencies. The cutoff includes older eligible entries in the selected currency. Review actual hours, billable hours and line fees. For a fixed fee, enter the agreed amount and choose whether actual hours appear on the customer document. You can reduce all fees or a selected set of people or lines by a percentage or to a target amount. Check the protected subtotal and final invoice total. The original recorded minutes remain in the time ledger.

Review service wording and the applicable billing rules, apply corrections and preview the draft. After editing a description or accepting a suggestion, preview the updated draft and run the AI wording check again to review the current text. New suggestions still require your acceptance. Links to entries open their service date and preserve the relevant matter and client; while the invoice editor stays open, **Continue invoice draft** returns to its existing inputs.

The approval screen displays the PDF rendered from the selected Word template. You can download the same PDF to inspect its letterhead, line items and total. Approval binds the line revisions, wording, amounts, recipient and template. A later edit requires a fresh preview and approval. Issue the approved revision to obtain the invoice number and download **Word** or **PDF**. If PDF conversion is interrupted, retry the invoice's document download. The existing reservation and number are reused.

## Design the Word template

Open **Billing settings → Firm → Invoice template**. Download the editable starter or upload your own DOCX (up to 1 MB). To design it with an agent, choose its workspace and open the template chat. The conversation starts with the setup skill and a working copy attached. Send the request, then continue editing that same copy in the chat or in Word. Return to Billing settings, load the edited workspace copy, preview the sample PDF and save the template. Future drafts use the new revision.

Place the invoice number, date, client name, address and total where they belong. Put the repeating block inside a single Word table row, with the description and amount in their respective cells. The setup skill supplies the supported Jinja2 expressions and handles the optional hours label. The local PDF renderer requires LibreOffice Writer. A host administrator may set `LEGALWORK_LIBREOFFICE_BINARY` to its executable. Issued Word and PDF files are stored together against the approved revision.

## When the firm exposes ordinary records only

A connected directory can support synchronized time entries and invoice records before it implements partner approval and issuance. In that configuration choose **Invoices → Record existing invoice**, enter the number and totals, and attach the existing PDF. This view cannot select a period's open services, obtain partner approval, assign an invoice number or produce Word and PDF from a template. Those actions require the firm's billing workflow. A record or attachment alone does not establish that its underlying services were invoiced. The people selector follows existing directory permissions. The planned hierarchy and billing delegations require a firm-side organization chart.
