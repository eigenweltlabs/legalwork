# Time tracking and billing

## Enter time and reconstruct a day

Open **Time tracking → Time entries** (called **My time** in the full workflow). The default workspace shows your booked work on the left and the selected day's timeline on the right. The tabs at the top work like the tabs in Wiki. Choose **+** (**New tab**) to open **Time entries**, **Day timeline** or **Private chat**. If the tab is already open, choosing it brings it into view. Use the tab’s **×** to close it. You can reopen it from **+**, including after closing every tab. Drag the divider to resize panes. Drag tabs to reorder or move them, or open a tab’s context menu to move it to another pane or split the pane. With a tab focused, Shift+Left or Shift+Right changes its position. Use Ctrl+Tab to switch tabs in the focused pane. On a small screen, **+** also takes you to tabs in another pane. Choose **+ → Reset workspace layout** to restore the initial arrangement. In **Week** and **Month**, the first figure is recorded time and the second is billable time. Select a date to open its daily entries and timeline.

Choose **+ New entry** at the left of the list. Enter the date, matter, service description and duration. A clock range is optional. The client usually follows from the matter. Choose the recipient when several clients apply. A complete manual entry follows the ordinary save and synchronization path. A row created for another date stays available under **Just created** while you finish this visit. The row appears under its service date when you return. Use the row selector and the usual copy and paste shortcuts to reuse an entry.

Open the gear next to **AI suggestions** to choose a model and activity sources. Turning suggestions on reconstructs the selected day when you open it. The optional schedule updates the current day. A connected model processes the selected evidence and relevant billing context. Private evidence, the conversation and rejected proposals stay outside the shared timesheet. A configured local model can process these on the device. If the model fails, select **Try again** in the workday to request a new draft for the same day with the selected model.

The timeline uses one color per matter and a separate color for unassigned work. Use **Timeline layout → By matter** for separate lanes. Untimed work appears separately. The red line marks the current time today. To review a suggestion with the keyboard, focus its calendar block and press Enter. Tab moves through the editor. Close the suggestion or press Escape to return to the timeline. The AI can group several events into one suggestion, divide work across matters or leave irrelevant activity out. Review the suggested narrative, matter, activity code, recorded time and billable time before accepting. The cross on the left rejects a suggestion. The check on the right books the edited entry. Use the existing-entry action when the same work was booked manually.

Open **Stopwatch** in the Time entries toolbar, choose a project or **No project**, and select **Start stopwatch**. Open it again to change the project or select **Stop stopwatch**. Its timer is shared with the floating desktop stopwatch. In AI settings, **Always show** offers a stopped timer for manual review. **Use as an AI source** lets the model combine the timer with other activity. On Home, activity follows the project selected in the composer. Choosing the general assistant ends the foreground observation for the previous project. An idle window and a running project agent provide separate observations. Completing a task in the desktop's task view provides evidence on the completion day, including for tasks without a due date.

Use **Private chat** to change the current draft. Save an instruction explicitly when it should affect future days. Pick its personal, matter or document scope. A document-specific correction such as “Call this document Revision” can guide later amendments. Creating an eligible project document provides DFT evidence and saving later changes provides MOD evidence. The model uses the effective wording rules, document history and available evidence when drafting the actual client-facing description.

## Billing settings and access

**Billing settings** contains the rules used to review and price entries, including effective dates, rates, increments, language and terminology. The AI settings also let you import billing instructions from TXT, DOCX, searchable PDF or structured JSON, review the extracted text and save a dated version for a client or matter. Device-only instructions belong to that account. Centrally managed billing policy and delegation depend on your firm's service.

Each saved entry has its own status. With the full workflow, submit a period from **My time**. An authorized reviewer can check hours, pricing, manual wording and suggested corrections. Issuing a bill links the included entries to the invoice. Approved changes to a previously issued entry use a correction linked to the original invoice.

## Create an invoice

In the full workflow, open **Billing review → Create Invoice** and choose the matter. Select a service period or include open work through a cutoff date. This includes older entries that became eligible after an earlier invoice. Resolve missing prices, approvals and competing fee agreements before issuing. When several billing clients apply, choose the recipient explicitly.

For an hourly invoice, choose the billing currency when the matter has fees in several currencies. The cutoff includes older eligible entries in the selected currency. Review actual hours, billable hours and line fees. For a fixed fee, enter the agreed amount and choose whether actual hours appear on the customer document. You can reduce all fees or a selected set of people or lines by a percentage or to a target amount. Check the protected subtotal and final invoice total. The original recorded minutes remain in the time ledger.

Review service wording and the applicable billing rules, apply corrections and preview the draft. The approval screen displays the PDF rendered from the selected Word template. You can download the same PDF to inspect its letterhead, line items and total. Approval binds the line revisions, wording, amounts, recipient and template. A later edit requires a fresh preview and approval. Issue the approved revision to obtain the invoice number and download **Word** or **PDF**. If PDF conversion is interrupted, retry the invoice's document download. The existing reservation and number are reused.

## Design the Word template

Open **Billing settings → Firm → Invoice template**. Download the editable starter or upload your own DOCX (up to 1 MB). To design it with an agent, choose its workspace and open the template chat. The conversation starts with the setup skill and a working copy attached. Send the request, then continue editing that same copy in the chat or in Word. Return to Billing settings, load the edited workspace copy, preview the sample PDF and save the template. Future drafts use the new revision.

Place the invoice number, date, client name, address and total where they belong. Put the repeating block inside a single Word table row, with the description and amount in their respective cells. The setup skill supplies the supported Jinja2 expressions and handles the optional hours label. The local PDF renderer requires LibreOffice Writer. A host administrator may set `LEGALWORK_LIBREOFFICE_BINARY` to its executable. Issued Word and PDF files are stored together against the approved revision.

## When the firm exposes ordinary records only

A connected directory can support synchronized time entries and invoice records before it implements partner approval and issuance. In that configuration **Invoices → Create Invoice** creates an ordinary record. The firm supplies invoice numbers, authoritative billed links, approval and final document storage through its billing service. A record or attachment alone does not establish that its underlying services were invoiced. The people selector follows existing directory permissions. The planned hierarchy and billing delegations require a firm-side organization chart.
