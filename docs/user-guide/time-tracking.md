# Time tracking and billing

Removing a saved time entry always asks for confirmation, whether it was entered manually or accepted from a suggestion. In the table, select the entries and choose **Archive**, review the listed entries, then choose **Remove** or **Cancel**. The same confirmation appears under **Record options > Archive record**. In the Timekeeping chat, the assistant requests a one-time approval before removing each saved entry. Automatic tool approval does not skip this step. If an entry changes while approval is pending, review its updated version before trying again. Removed entries retain their history. Unaccepted AI suggestions can be revised or dismissed without this deletion confirmation; they stay private until accepted.

## Enter time and reconstruct a day

Open **Time tracking**. The default workspace puts **Time entries** on the left, with **Invoices** beside it when you have billing access, and the selected day's timeline on the right. Choose the date and **Day**, **Week** or **Month** in the window header. The window’s **Back** and **Forward** buttons restore earlier dates and ranges. **Stopwatch** starts a matter timer from the same header. Each pane has the same tab strip as Wiki, aligned with its content. Choose **+** (**New tab**) to open **Time entries**, **Invoices** or **Day timeline**. Choose **New chat** to start a fresh conversation, or **Files…** to open a workspace document in a tab. If the tab is already open, choosing it brings it into view. Use the tab’s **×** to close it. You can reopen it from **+**, including after closing every tab. Drag the divider to resize panes. Drag tabs to reorder or move them, or open a tab’s context menu to move it to another pane or split the pane. With a tab focused, Shift+Left or Shift+Right changes its position. Use Ctrl+Tab to switch tabs in the focused pane. On a small screen, **+** also takes you to tabs in another pane. Choose **+ → Reset workspace layout** to restore the initial arrangement. In **Week** and **Month**, the first figure is recorded time and the second is billable time. Select a date to open its daily entries and timeline.

Use **New entry** at the upper right above the table. **Submit period** is beside it when the firm supports approvals. Enter the date, matter, service description and duration. Clock times are optional. Time can be recorded and suggestions accepted as soon as you choose a matter. A single linked client is filled in automatically. When several clients apply or the matter has no client yet, the time and billable minutes are saved with the recipient open. Entries saved within the displayed period appear among the other rows. An entry for a different period stays available under **Just created** until you leave the date, with a link to its service date. **Recorded** and **Billable** totals appear beneath their columns and follow the table filters. Use **Search records** or **Filter** as in Back Office. Under **Options**, select a field to show its column or clear it to hide it. Newly shown columns scroll into view. In the records table, **Show archive** opens archived entries. Select an entry and choose **Restore** to bring it back. When the firm authorizes recording for colleagues, the timekeeper control appears in the app bar. A new row uses the selected person.

In **Day timeline**, choose **Run suggestions** in the calendar header. Check the date in the dialog inside **Day timeline**, then select **Create suggestions**. You can keep working in the **Time entries** pane while the dialog is open. The agent reads the enabled activity for the selected day, so it can group related work. The agent works in the background while **Day timeline** stays open. Select **Chat** to follow its progress and open past runs. Timekeeping chats are available here. The agent checks existing entries, reads the guidelines for each matter, and publishes suggestions as it finishes them. You can edit, dismiss or book each published draft. Open **Chat** and select **Stop** to interrupt the review. Drafts already published remain available. A completed check counts even if it found no work to suggest. Opening a date displays previously saved results.

The Day timeline shows project appointments and the connected calendars visible in **Calendar** as soon as you select a date. Click an appointment and choose **Create time entry**. Review its description and times, select a matter, and save. For an all-day appointment, enter the minutes you worked. In **By matter** view, appointments have their own **Calendar** lane.

To record work directly in the timeline, drag from the start to the end of an empty time range. Dragging up also works. A single click starts a 30-minute entry. The selection snaps to 15 minutes, and you can adjust the exact start and end in the form. Starting in a matter’s column fills in that matter. After you save, timed entries appear on the timeline and in **Time entries**.

Under **Time entries > Options > Time format**, choose **Hours:minutes (hh:mm)** (the default) or **Minutes**. In both modes, typing `38` means 38 minutes. In Hours:minutes, typing `138` becomes `1:38` (98 minutes) when you leave the field; in Minutes it stays `138` (138 minutes). You can also type `1:38` explicitly. For three or more digits in Hours:minutes, the last two digits are the minute part and must be 00–59. The choice applies to Recorded and Billable inputs, suggestion editors, table totals and the small Week/Month calendar. Switching formats changes the display of existing durations, never their stored time: an existing 138-minute entry displays `2:18` in Hours:minutes. The choice is remembered on this device.

Calendar blocks use the same matter color as the Matter field in Time entries, in both light and dark themes. Suggestions and recorded entries for that matter share the color; unassigned work stays neutral. Record and task identity colors exclude grey, white and black. Suggestion cards show the matter label and service description together on the first line, with the activity source and its icon in grey underneath. Stopwatch intervals and suggestions without clock times use this same layout. In the calendar header, switch from **All matters** to **By matter** to see each matter in its own lane. **Unassigned matter** appears only when a visible item needs a matter. **Expand day timeline** fills the workspace inside the current window. Choose **Restore workspace** or press Escape to return to your split layout. **Suggestions without a clock time** stay in a pinned banner at the top while you scroll through the hours. The banner appears only when there are untimed items; all-day appointments share this area. If there are many items, scroll within the banner to review them without losing your place in the timeline. Use **−** and **+** immediately to the left of **Expand day timeline** in the calendar header, pinch on the trackpad, or hold Ctrl/Cmd while scrolling to zoom the time scale. Short entries keep their readable minimum height; longer intervals grow with the scale. Zooming preserves the time under the pointer and does not change recorded minutes. The scale is remembered when you change days. The timeline limits how far you can zoom out so it stays scrollable when you resize the window. The red line marks the current time today. To review a suggestion with the keyboard, focus its calendar block and press Enter. Tab moves through the editor. Close the suggestion or press Escape to return to the timeline. The AI can group several events into one suggestion, divide work across matters or leave irrelevant activity out. Review the suggested narrative, matter, activity code, recorded time and billable time before accepting. Each calendar block has a shared pill with a cross on the left to reject it and a check on the right to book it. When required details are missing, the check opens the editor. A distinctive filename, appointment title or exact matter reference can identify a matter. A shared client name or an app name such as Finder can leave the matter unassigned. Separate activities can overlap. Where related observations support an estimate, the suggestion shows estimated minutes for you to review. Use the existing-entry action when the same work was booked manually.

You can also ask the Timekeeping chat to edit an unaccepted suggestion, for example “Set this suggestion to 20 recorded minutes.” This updates the same private suggestion, including one you have already edited, and retains its source evidence. You can ask the chat to remove an unaccepted suggestion that has only been edited by the AI. Changes you make yourself in the suggestion editor protect it from removal by the agent, including after later AI edits. Accepted suggestions require the same removal confirmation as manual entries. It does not accept the suggestion, create a duplicate or change a manual time entry. An edit conflicts if someone changes or books that suggestion before it is saved; the chat must read the current version before trying again. Saved edits update the calendar block’s description, matter and color. Enter **Recorded** minutes directly in the suggestion editor, including for a document without a known duration. The edit stays private until you accept the suggestion. If you change its duration, the booking becomes an entry without clock times; the original event remains available as source evidence. Open the block in **Day timeline** and choose **Edit** to set the start and end. Choose an activity by name, such as **Research · RES**, **Drafting · DFT**, or **Amending · MOD**, then click **Save**. The code identifies the type of work in billing. If the agent cannot identify the activity, the draft leaves it open for you to choose. A timed suggestion can still show its recorded minutes. A matter’s billing rules can supply different labels. Correcting the source preserves unrelated edits, such as a client selection or reviewed billable time. Changing the duration, matter or activity recalculates the fields that depend on that change. If another window changes a draft before you correct or accept it, review the updated values and confirm again. Text you are still typing stays in the editor during a background refresh.

Open **Settings → Time tracking → Suggestion schedule** and set **Repeat** to **Never** to stop automatic runs. Activity capture continues, and you can select **Run suggestions** in **Day timeline** for any past day or today. To schedule suggestions, select **Every hour** or **Daily**. The daily default is 18:00 local time. You can change the daily run times and add more. LegalWork must be running for a scheduled check. Each scheduled chat reviews today and checks the previous day for uncovered work. It proposes measured or estimated minutes where the activity supports them. Select the model in the chat composer.

Turning off **Time tracking** hides it in the sidebar and pauses capture and suggestions. New accounts and upgrades use tasks, documents, workspace activity, saved meeting recordings and supported connected integrations by default. Your saved opt-outs carry forward. Turn on **Desktop activity** to capture activity from other apps.

Open **Stopwatch** at the right of the Time header, optionally choose a matter or add a quick note, then select **Start stopwatch**. Open it again to change the matter for the entire interval or select **Stop stopwatch**. Its timer is shared with the floating desktop stopwatch. After you stop the timer, it appears in **Day timeline** with its recorded times and quick note. Open it to review and save it as a time entry. On Home, activity follows the project selected in the composer. Choosing the general assistant ends the foreground observation for the previous project. The day log contains the document or chat title, clock times, elapsed time, and excerpts of submitted chat requests and replies. A document left open in another tab is marked as background. **Foreground agent** means the running chat was focused in the active LegalWork window. Switching to a document, another chat or another app makes that run **Background agent**. A run can have both labels at different times. Agent runtime provides context for suggestions. Only separately observed human activity supports measured work time. Older runs without focus information show **Agent focus unknown**. Control this source in **Settings → Time tracking → Workspace activity**. Completing a task in the desktop's task view provides evidence on the completion day, including for tasks without a due date.

Project calendar invitations use the organizer, guest responses and assignment to identify your meetings. A decline on one date skips that occurrence of a recurring meeting. The **Project documents saved in LegalWork** source includes edits and restored revisions in Wiki, attached files and project contributions you publish. These actions give the agent the filename and time. Review any suggested duration before booking.

Connect mail, calendars and file storage in **Settings → Integrations**. Time suggestions automatically use supported connected accounts. Google Drive reports your latest edit to an accessible file. OneDrive for work or school reports changes you made in your own drive. SharePoint checks document libraries for edits attributed to your Microsoft account. Provider indexes can take time to update. OneDrive Personal starts watching at its first successful check after you connect it. Select **Create suggestions** once to start it. Each later check preserves edits it observes, including on another device. A file change supplies a title and timestamp. Opening, owning or syncing a file does not by itself establish time worked.

**Document saves** also records explicit project imports, copies and inbox attachments. Files you upload or save through a project’s storage connection, including Box and Dropbox, are captured when that operation succeeds. Changes made directly in the Box or Dropbox apps are outside this source. **Tasks** captures your task creations, edits, completions, notes and task attachments in LegalWork. Notes written by you on another device appear after the firm’s task sync. A task’s due date, assignment, sync timestamp or another person’s note provides no evidence that you worked on it. External task services such as Google Tasks and Microsoft To Do have no native activity connector.

Start a recording in LegalWork. With **Meeting recordings** enabled in **Settings → Time tracking → Activity sources**, the next check can propose time from the recording title, linked project and capture interval. Review the matter and minutes before booking. Imported audio, dictations, transcripts and summaries stay outside this source.

### Use activity from this computer

In the desktop app, open **Settings → Time tracking → Activity sources** and turn on **Desktop activity**. Leave LegalWork running while working in another app. The setting shows when LegalWork last sampled the desktop. Idle time, a locked screen and a sleeping computer leave gaps. Turning off **Desktop activity** stops collection and excludes this source from new suggestion runs.

To review an earlier day, open it in **Day timeline** and choose **Run suggestions**, then **Create suggestions**. The agent uses desktop activity recorded on that day along with your other enabled sources.

On macOS the active app can supply its name. Choose **Grant Accessibility access** to let LegalWork include the filename when an app exposes a local document. On Windows the live source records the foreground application. The private log contains app or document names and observation times. A suggestion run can use those observations with the model selected for timekeeping. Review the suggested matter and duration before booking.

Open **Chat** for the day’s conversation, with the same composer, model picker, skills, attachments and tool results as a project chat. Today’s conversation opens first. Scroll up for older days. The timekeeping project belongs to the current profile. You can also ask the main Assistant to delegate work to the timekeeping chat. Suggested prompts start ordinary requests. To reuse a practice or instruction, ask the agent to put it into a project skill.

You can ask **Chat** to translate billing descriptions, correct time, or remove saved entries for a specific day. Name the date and identify the rows, for example “Translate yesterday’s Kestrel entries into German and keep the minutes.” The chat checks each row’s revision. For a removal, the confirmation appears in that chat, including the embedded Timekeeping chat, whether the entry was entered manually or accepted from an AI suggestion. Choose **Allow once** to remove the displayed entry or **Deny** to keep it. Without a decision, the entry stays saved. Removed table records appear in the archive. In the approval workflow, withdrawing a pending or confirmed entry removes it from the active timesheet and records the change in its history. Approved or invoiced time follows the billing correction workflow.

## Billing settings and access

In **Settings → Billing settings**, open **Time rules** to manage the firm’s billing policy or **Review** to assign a reviewer. Select the policy level and effective date, then choose an **Assigned reviewer**. Billing settings save automatically. Switches and dropdowns save when changed, and text and number fields save when you leave the field. In the full workflow, billing managers can change these settings. Other members can inspect the rules their account can access. The **Billing guidelines** tab contains the guidelines saved to your account on this device. In records mode, **Time rules** links to **Fee agreements** in Back Office.

In the booking form, type a matter name or reference into **Matter** and select a match. You can show the optional **Project** and **Activity** columns from the time-entry grid’s column options.

In the basic **Time entries** view, you can continue recording for yourself when the people list cannot load. Your own complete entries enter the local outbox when the firm connection is interrupted and synchronize when it returns. Recording for another person still requires the firm's current permission; a previously loaded people list does not grant access during an outage.

### Link a billing guideline

1. Open **Settings → Billing settings → Billing guidelines**, or choose **Billing guidelines** from a client or matter record in Back Office.
2. From a client or matter record, the link shows the guidelines for that record. If it has none yet, the form opens with the record selected. In general settings, choose **New guideline**, enter a name and set the scope. **Firm** covers eligible matters. **Client** lets you pick a client from Back Office. **Matter** lets you pick one matter. Choose the effective date.
3. Enter the instructions or import a TXT, DOCX, searchable PDF or JSON file. **Extract time rules** proposes numerical rules with supporting quotations. Check the extracted wording, minimum, maximum, rounding and billing increment. You can also enter these yourself.
4. Choose **Save guideline version**. The list shows each saved guideline’s name, linked scope, effective date and revision. Open a guideline to edit it or follow the link to its client or matter. The profile stores the reviewed text and rules on this device. Importing a document leaves its original file in place.

Guidelines are private to the current account on this device, including a guideline with **Firm** scope. The service selects the applicable dated version for the firm, client and matter and combines their rules by field. A client guideline applies automatically when the matter identifies one visible billing client. A matter with multiple clients needs an explicit recipient for client-specific invoice review. Expand **Rules for a matter** and search by matter name or reference. Choose the service date to see its minimum, maximum, billing increment, rounding and rule sources. The agent uses the wording to draft suggestions. The records grid uses the time rules for manual billable minutes. Shared pricing, invoice approvals and binding terms come from **Billing settings** and the fee agreement. Published rules take precedence for fields they set.

For example, a reviewed six-minute rounding rule can suggest **17 Recorded / 18 Billable** without changing the actual duration. Acceptance also checks the billing service's rules. If that service requires ten-minute increments, the suggestion cannot be booked as 18 minutes: have the authorized billing administrator correct the applicable policy or review the proposed billable time. A private guideline does not change the service's policy. After resolving a failed acceptance, **Try again** retries that acceptance; retrying a failed private edit keeps the suggestion private.

Each saved entry has its own status. With the full workflow, save each entry in **Time entries**. When you save your own billable time without an assigned reviewer, it shows as **Approved** once it has a billing client. Go straight to **Invoices → Unbilled time** to bill it. Entries marked **Ready to submit** go to the assigned reviewer when you select **Submit period**. An authorized reviewer can check hours, pricing, manual wording and suggested corrections. Issuing a bill links the included entries to the invoice. Approved changes to a previously issued entry use a correction linked to the original invoice.

In a shared workspace, open **Review** and choose the service date or range. Click anywhere on an entry’s row to open its review. You can approve the entry as shown, edit its description or billable time and approve it in one step, or return it to the contributor. Choose **Save review** to keep your edits pending. Billing rules apply to billable time. A reason is optional. Contributors can edit their own open entries. Reviewers edit other contributors’ submitted time in **Review**. In **Review** and **Time entries**, **Show revision history** opens the history sidebar in the current tab. Use the checkboxes to select multiple entries for **Approve selection**.

## Create an invoice

1. Open **Invoices** and select **Create Invoice**. You can also open **Unbilled time** and choose **Invoice this entry** beside a row to start with it selected.
2. Search for the **Matter**. If the matter has one billing client, LegalWork fills it in. When it has several, choose the **Client**.
3. Check the time entries to invoice. Use **Filter by date** to narrow the list. **Entries outside this invoice** shows entries awaiting review or missing a billing client.
4. Choose **Create Invoice** to save the numbered invoice in **All invoices** and mark the selected time as invoiced. For hourly work, LegalWork uses the saved rates. If a rate is missing, choose **Edit rate** and **Save rate**. For a flat-fee agreement, select **Use fixed price** and enter the agreed amount.
5. For a document, ask the Timekeeping agent to create it from the saved invoice number. It can read the invoice’s recipient, amounts and time entries.

In **Invoices → Unbilled time**, check **Status** to see which entries are waiting for approval. Select **Review** on a row assigned to you to open that entry’s review form and approve it or return it to the timekeeper. Fees are shown only for entries you can invoice. Select **Show revision history** in the row to open the history sidebar.

For an imported, recorded entry you own, follow the link to **Time entries**, edit and save it, then return to the invoice. An approved imported record cannot be added directly. If an entry needs a billing client, choose **Assign to [client]** when offered. Assigning a recipient to a previously approved entry returns it to review under that client's billing terms.

In **All invoices**, open an invoice row to see its billed **Time entries**. Select an entry to open its record. You can also open an attachment from the invoice record. Connected firms need the current billing service to create invoices from time entries. If the firm connection drops while editing, choose **Try again** to restore access and continue with the same selection.

## Set timekeeper rates

Open **Settings → Billing settings → Time rules → Fees**. Choose the **Policy level** and **Effective from** date. Use **Default hourly rate** for the policy’s default rate. For an individual rate, choose the **Contributor**, select **Add contributor rate** and enter the rate on their row. Set the **Currency** there as well. The setting saves when you leave the field. For your own entries without an assigned reviewer, the invoice uses the rate and currency effective on the service date. Entries approved by a reviewer use the rate saved at approval.

## When the firm exposes ordinary records only

A connected directory can support synchronized time entries and invoice records before it supports creating invoices from time entries. In the **Invoices** tab, **More actions → Team sync** opens inside the pane. You can keep using the neighboring chat or timeline while reviewing sync details. Choose **Close dialog** when you are done. In that configuration choose **Invoices → Record existing invoice**, enter the number and totals, and attach the existing PDF. Creating a numbered invoice from saved time requires the firm's billing workflow. A record or attachment alone does not establish that its underlying services were invoiced. The people selector follows existing directory permissions. The planned hierarchy and billing delegations require a firm-side organization chart.

The floating bar shows the stopwatch beside the microphone. In the floating stopwatch or the Time header, search by matter name or reference, choose a match if you want to assign the time, and enter an optional **Quick note**. You can start and stop a personal timer with the matter blank. Booking the interval into the timesheet requires a matter. The note becomes the description of the stopped timer. You can update it when you stop the timer.

The Timekeeping chat can save time, submit it to an assigned reviewer, and create an invoice record. It follows your account’s access and uses the current record revisions. When you ask it to create an invoice, confirm the change in the app.
